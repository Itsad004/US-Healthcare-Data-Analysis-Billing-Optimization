# US Healthcare Data Analysis & Billing Optimization

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing 10,000 US healthcare patient records using Microsoft Excel to identify healthcare trends, understand patient demographics, and evaluate medical billing patterns.

The analysis uses data cleaning, data validation, Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), PivotTables, and statistical comparisons to generate meaningful business insights.

## Objectives

- Analyse patient demographics based on age and gender.
- Identify the most common medical conditions across demographic groups.
- Examine average billing amounts across different medical conditions.
- Compare healthcare billing across insurance providers.
- Analyse admission trends by year, month, and day.
- Generate insights to support healthcare cost analysis and optimisation.

## Tools & Technologies

## Dataset Overview

The project uses a publicly available US healthcare dataset containing 10,000 patient records.

Key attributes include:

- Patient demographics: Age, Gender, Blood Type
- Medical information: Medical Condition
- Admission details: Date of Admission, Admission Type
- Healthcare details: Doctor, Hospital, Insurance Provider
- Financial information: Billing Amount

## Analysis Performed

### 1. Data Cleaning & Preparation
- Reviewed and prepared the dataset for analysis.
- Standardised demographic categories.
- Created age groups and demographic classifications.
- Prepared additional date-related fields for trend analysis.

### 2. Demographic Analysis
- Analysed patient distribution by age and gender.
- Created six age-gender demographic groups.
- Examined medical condition frequency across demographic groups.

### 3. Medical Condition Analysis
- Compared the frequency of different medical conditions.
- Identified patterns across patient demographic groups.

### 4. Healthcare Billing Analysis
- Compared average billing amounts across medical conditions.
- Evaluated billing differences among insurance providers.
- Identified conditions associated with higher average healthcare costs.

### 5. Admission Trend Analysis
- Analysed admissions by year, month, and day of the week.
- Examined monthly and yearly patient admission patterns.

## Key Business Insights

- Hypertension was the most frequently observed medical condition, affecting 2,155 patients (21.55%).
- Cancer recorded the highest average billing amount at approximately $39,688.
- Obesity recorded the lowest average billing among the analysed conditions, at approximately $12,500.
- Cigna recorded the highest average billing among the compared insurance providers, at approximately $24,224.
- Blue Cross recorded the lowest average billing, at approximately $22,773.
